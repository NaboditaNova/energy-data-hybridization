\# Energy Data Hybridization



Python-based workflows for processing, mapping, and hybridizing energy statistics for European and non-European countries.



\## Project Overview



This repository is being organized as a portfolio project demonstrating scientific data processing, harmonization, mapping, and validation using Python.



\## Project Structure



\- `eu\_efta/` — Eurostat energy balance and physical energy flow data processing and hybridization.

\- `non\_eu/` — Non-EU energy data processing workflows (to be added).



\## EU/EFTA Workflow



The EU/EFTA workflow combines Eurostat energy balance data (`nrg\_bal\_c`) with physical energy flow supply-use data (`env\_ac\_pefasu`).



The workflow consists of five stages:



1\. Process Eurostat energy balance data.

2\. Map energy balance data.

3\. Process Eurostat PSUT energy data.

4\. Map PSUT energy data.

5\. Hybridize energy balance and PSUT data.
